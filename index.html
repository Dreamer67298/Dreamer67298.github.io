The Simple Process

Two photos.
A fresh perspective.

You don't need to manually cut out clothing or learn professional photo editing. Start with two good images, then let the virtual try-on workflow do its work.

01

Start with your photo

Upload one well-lit person photo with the clothes and body position visible. A full-body shot is useful when changing a complete outfit.

02

Bring a garment reference

Add one image of the jacket, dress or outfit you want to explore. Product photos, flat lays and on-model references can all work.

03

Generate your try-on

Add optional styling instructions, choose Low, Medium or High quality, and review your result before downloading it.

Little Details Matter

Better inputs.
Better outfit ideas.

A few careful choices can help the AI understand which clothes to replace and how the new outfit should look.

◎

Use a clear person photo

Keep the subject well lit with the outfit visible. Try to avoid crossed arms, busy mirrors and clothes hidden behind bags or hair.

▧

Show the entire garment

A sharp product image or flat lay with visible hems, sleeves and patterns gives the model more dependable details to work from.

✎

Keep instructions specific

Optional notes such as “leave the jacket open” or “keep the bag unchanged” can help clarify what you want to see.

✓

Inspect before publishing

Check hands, logos, fabric texture and garment proportions. An AI preview is not a guarantee of real-world sizing or exact product appearance.

Questions & Answers

The details
worth knowing.

A practical guide to how AI clothes changing works, what to upload and what a digital preview can—and can't—show.

What is AI ClothChange?

AI ClothChange is an online AI clothes changer. It takes one person photo and one garment reference, then creates an edited image to preview the clothing on the person.

Can I change clothes using only one image?

The current workflow requires two inputs: one image of a person and one image of the garment. The clothing reference may be a product photo, flat lay or on-model image.

Which image file formats are supported?

The main tool accepts JPG, PNG, WebP and AVIF images for both the person and clothing reference.

Will the result keep my face and background?

The tool aims to preserve the person's identity, pose, lighting and scene while changing their outfit. The result can vary with source-image quality and clothing complexity.

Can a virtual try-on tell me my exact clothing size?

No. It helps visualize style, proportions and silhouette, not real measurements or how a fabric will feel. Check the retailer's sizing information before purchasing.

Is it suitable for commercial or product content?

Use images and likenesses you are authorized to use, and verify the garment's appearance before publishing a commercial image. Review AI ClothChange's privacy policy and applicable terms for more information.
